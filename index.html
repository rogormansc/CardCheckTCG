BUILT FOR THE DEMO

A clearer card check.

CardCheck is a student prototype with visual feature matching for a catalog of up to eight cards. It compares local image details and checks their geometric arrangement. A strong match is evidence, not a calibrated probability.

Confirm the card’s name, number, set, and variant yourself. Recognition does not establish authenticity, language, print edition, condition, or investment value.

All displayed prices are fictional examples in USD. They demonstrate the planned comparison interface. Condition is entered by you, and it does not automatically change sample prices.

Your scans never leave the device. First-time reference downloads and external marketplace links require internet access. No accounts, analytics, or paid AI APIs are included.

Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost. On iPhone, open the HTTPS site in Safari, then use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Pokémon and its artwork belong to their respective rights holders. This independent project does not grant reuse rights for artwork. See SETUP.md for deployment and image-use notes.
